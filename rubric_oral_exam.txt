ORAL EXAMINATION EVALUATION RUBRIC

Grade the oral defense on a 4-point scale for each category:

1. UNDERSTANDING OF OWN WORK (0-4 points)
   4 = Deep understanding; can explain all aspects confidently
   3 = Good understanding; minor gaps
   2 = Partial understanding; struggles with some aspects
   1 = Limited understanding; often confused
   0 = Does not understand their own work

   Evaluate:
   - Can they explain their arguments without reading?
   - Do they understand the implications of their claims?
   - Can they connect different parts of their work?
   - Do they recognize the significance of their findings?

2. REASONING & JUSTIFICATION (0-4 points)
   4 = Excellent reasoning; clear justification for all choices
   3 = Good reasoning; justifies most decisions
   2 = Weak reasoning; struggles to explain choices
   1 = Poor reasoning; cannot justify decisions
   0 = No coherent reasoning

   Evaluate:
   - Can they explain WHY they made certain choices?
   - Do their explanations make logical sense?
   - Can they defend their positions under questioning?
   - Do they provide evidence for their reasoning?

3. CONSIDERATION OF ALTERNATIVES (0-4 points)
   4 = Thoroughly considered alternatives; aware of trade-offs
   3 = Considered some alternatives; understands main trade-offs
   2 = Limited consideration; vague about alternatives
   1 = Did not consider alternatives
   0 = Unaware alternatives exist

   Evaluate:
   - Can they describe what else they considered?
   - Do they understand why they rejected alternatives?
   - Are they aware of the strengths/weaknesses of their approach?
   - Can they discuss trade-offs intelligently?

OVERALL ORAL EXAM SCORE: Average of three categories (0-4.0)
