EXAM CONTEXT - Machine Learning Project

TOPIC: Supervised Learning and Model Evaluation

The student has completed a course project on building a classification model to predict customer churn. They explored various algorithms including logistic regression, decision trees, and random forests.

Key topics covered in the course:
- Supervised vs unsupervised learning
- Classification vs regression
- Train/test splits and cross-validation
- Overfitting and underfitting
- Model evaluation metrics (accuracy, precision, recall, F1, AUC-ROC)
- Feature engineering and selection
- Ensemble methods
- Bias-variance tradeoff

EXAM FOCUS:
Test the student's understanding of:
1. Why they chose specific models for their project
2. How they evaluated model performance
3. What trade-offs they considered
4. What could go wrong with their approach
5. How they would explain their model to non-technical stakeholders
