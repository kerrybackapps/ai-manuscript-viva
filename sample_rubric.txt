GRADING RUBRIC - Machine Learning Fundamentals

Evaluate the student on a 4-point scale for each category:
4 = Excellent: Deep understanding, can explain nuances and trade-offs
3 = Good: Solid understanding, can explain concepts correctly
2 = Fair: Basic understanding, some gaps or misconceptions
1 = Poor: Limited understanding, significant gaps
0 = No evidence of understanding

CATEGORIES:

1. Conceptual Understanding (Weight: 35%)
   - Can the student explain core ML concepts clearly?
   - Do they understand the underlying mathematics/theory?
   - Can they distinguish between related concepts?

2. Practical Application (Weight: 25%)
   - Can the student apply concepts to real scenarios?
   - Do they understand when to use different techniques?
   - Can they reason about model choices?

3. Critical Thinking (Weight: 25%)
   - Does the student recognize limitations and failure modes?
   - Can they identify potential issues or biases?
   - Do they consider trade-offs and alternatives?

4. Communication (Weight: 15%)
   - Can the student explain technical concepts clearly?
   - Do they use appropriate terminology?
   - Can they provide concrete examples?

OVERALL SCORE:
Calculate weighted average and round to 1 decimal place (0.0 - 4.0 scale)
