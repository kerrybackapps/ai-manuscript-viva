MANUSCRIPT EVALUATION RUBRIC

Grade the written manuscript on a 4-point scale for each category:

1. CLARITY (0-4 points)
   4 = Exceptionally clear; ideas expressed precisely; no ambiguity
   3 = Generally clear; minor unclear passages
   2 = Somewhat unclear; reader must work to understand
   1 = Frequently unclear; confusing presentation
   0 = Incomprehensible

   Evaluate:
   - Are sentences well-constructed?
   - Is terminology used correctly?
   - Are concepts explained clearly?
   - Is the writing free of jargon or properly defined?

2. COHERENCE (0-4 points)
   4 = Highly coherent; logical flow throughout; strong transitions
   3 = Generally coherent; mostly logical structure
   2 = Somewhat disjointed; some logical gaps
   1 = Poorly organized; difficult to follow
   0 = Incoherent structure

   Evaluate:
   - Does the argument flow logically?
   - Are sections well-connected?
   - Do transitions guide the reader?
   - Is there a clear through-line?

3. COMPREHENSIVENESS (0-4 points)
   4 = Thoroughly addresses all aspects; excellent depth
   3 = Addresses most aspects adequately
   2 = Missing some important elements
   1 = Superficial treatment; major gaps
   0 = Severely incomplete

   Evaluate:
   - Does it address the assignment requirements?
   - Is the scope appropriate?
   - Are claims supported with evidence?
   - Are counterarguments considered?

OVERALL MANUSCRIPT SCORE: Average of three categories (0-4.0)
