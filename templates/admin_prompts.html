ℹ️ About Prompts

Manuscript Analysis: This prompt is sent to Claude to analyze the student's manuscript before the oral exam. It identifies key claims, methodology, gaps, and generates targeted question areas.

Examiner Agent: This prompt configures the ElevenLabs AI agent that conducts the oral examination. It defines the agent's behavior, questioning strategy, and examination style.

Manuscript Grading Rubric: Defines how to grade manuscript quality based on clarity, coherence, and comprehensiveness.

Oral Exam Grading Rubric: Defines how to grade oral examination performance based on understanding, reasoning, and consideration of alternatives.
