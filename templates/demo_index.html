How It Works

  • Step 1: Upload your manuscript (or use a sample)
  • Step 2: AI reads and analyzes your work
  • Step 3: Custom examiner agent is created
  • Step 4: Take the oral exam (voice-based)
  • Step 5: Receive grades from 3-model grading council

Grading System

Part A - Manuscript Quality (4-point scale):

  • Clarity: Precision of language and explanations
  • Coherence: Logical structure and flow
  • Comprehensiveness: Depth of coverage and evidence

Part B - Oral Examination (4-point scale):

  • Understanding: Can explain and defend your work
  • Reasoning: Clear justification for choices
  • Alternatives: Awareness of other approaches and trade-offs

Each part is graded independently by Claude Sonnet 4.5, OpenAI o1, and Gemini 2.0 Flash.
